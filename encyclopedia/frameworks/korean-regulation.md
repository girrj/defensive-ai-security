# Korean breach reporting and security certification (국내 신고·인증 제도)

## Definition (정의)

Korean breach reporting and certification (국내 신고·인증 제도) span the Network Act, PIPA and sector-specific requirements.

## Why it matters

An incident can trigger more than one duty, and incident reporting is distinct from individual notice and certification.

## How to do it / what to check

Record awareness time and entity role; check the law in force for the case date, thresholds, exceptions and recipient; coordinate notifications without assuming one filing satisfies all duties.

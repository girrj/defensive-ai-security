# OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026

## Definition (정의)

OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026 (LLM 상위 위험) is a community risk guide for LLM-powered applications.

## Why it matters

It frames risks such as untrusted content, data exposure, supply chain and excessive agency for application owners.

## How to do it / what to check

Map each applicable risk to a trust boundary, prevention control, logging and an authorized test; assess actual exposure instead of counting checklist items.

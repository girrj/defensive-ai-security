# MITRE D3FEND

## Definition (정의)

MITRE D3FEND (방어 기법 지식베이스) describes defensive techniques and relationships to offensive behavior.

## Why it matters

It helps turn a threat description into candidate controls without implying that a control is deployed or effective.

## How to do it / what to check

Choose a defensive technique, identify its required data and owner, implement a local control and test it against benign and expected-positive examples.

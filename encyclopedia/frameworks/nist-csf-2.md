# NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

## Definition (정의)

NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 (사이버보안 프레임워크) organizes outcomes under Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover.

## Why it matters

The functions let management compare risk decisions and operational coverage without treating a checklist as certification.

## How to do it / what to check

Create a current and target profile, assign owners and evidence for outcomes, and revisit priorities after incidents and material changes.

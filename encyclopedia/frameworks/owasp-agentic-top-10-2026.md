# OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026

## Definition (정의)

OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026 (에이전트 상위 위험) addresses systems that plan and use tools with delegated authority.

## Why it matters

A tool action can alter data or systems even when model text looks harmless.

## How to do it / what to check

Inventory tools, identities and data scopes; enforce authorization outside the model; limit delegated power and require review for consequential actions.

# Cyber Kill Chain, Diamond Model, Pyramid of Pain

## Definition (정의)

The Cyber Kill Chain (사이버 킬체인), Diamond Model (다이아몬드 모델) and Pyramid of Pain (고통의 피라미드) are complementary analytic lenses: phase, event relationships and indicator durability.

## Why it matters

They help organize evidence and prioritize behavior-based detections, but real incidents need not follow one sequence.

## How to do it / what to check

Use the case timeline as evidence, link actor/infrastructure/capability/victim only where supported, and prefer controls on repeatable behaviors over short-lived hashes.

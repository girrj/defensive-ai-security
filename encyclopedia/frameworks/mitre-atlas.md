# MITRE ATLAS

## Definition (정의)

MITRE ATLAS (AI 위협 지식베이스) catalogs adversarial AI tactics and techniques for AI-enabled systems.

## Why it matters

AI application threats cross model, data and tool boundaries that conventional host-only coverage can miss.

## How to do it / what to check

Map concrete assets and trust boundaries to relevant ATLAS techniques; test data provenance, model access and tool authorization; record unsupported mappings as gaps.

# NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3

## Definition (정의)

NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 (침해사고 대응 권고) is a CSF 2.0 community profile published in April 2025.

## Why it matters

It places incident response in organization-wide risk management and supersedes the older revision 2 guide.

## How to do it / what to check

Align Govern, Identify and Protect preparation with Detect, Respond and Recover activities; define roles, decision authority, evidence and improvement feedback.

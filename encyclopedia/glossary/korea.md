# Glossary: Korean regulation and institutions (국내 법·제도·기관)

This is a navigation glossary, not a legal determination. Apply the law in force on the incident's relevant date and check the organization's status and the facts. The linked statute and ordinance texts were checked on 2026-11-15 for the current framework; case-specific legal advice needs separate review.

| Term | 한국어 | Definition | See also |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Network Act incident reporting | 정보통신망법 침해사고 신고 | An information and communications service provider assesses reporting under Article 48-3; the current text requires initial reporting within 24 hours of awareness, with separate follow-up duties in the ordinance. | [IR manual](../../ir-manual/README.md) |
| PIPA breach report and notice | 개인정보 보호법 유출 신고·통지 | A personal information controller separately assesses notice to individuals and reporting to the authority under Article 34 and its ordinance; applicable conditions and exceptions matter. | [IR manual](../../ir-manual/README.md) |
| Credit Information Act | 신용정보법 | Statute governing personal credit information and credit information businesses; financial incidents need its own applicability review. | [Case 13](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/13-financial-sector-incidents-2026.md) |
| Electronic Financial Transactions Act | 전자금융거래법 | Statute covering electronic financial transactions and related responsibilities; verify entity and service scope. | [Case 13](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/13-financial-sector-incidents-2026.md) |
| ISMS-P | 정보보호 및 개인정보보호 관리체계 인증 | Korean certification combining security and personal-information management requirements; certification does not replace incident response. | [IR manual](../../ir-manual/README.md) |
| Network separation | 망분리 | Segregating networks and access paths according to applicable sector rules and risk; test allowed flows and remote access. | [Case 13](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/13-financial-sector-incidents-2026.md) |
| GPKI / administrative electronic signature | 행정전자서명 | Government public-key infrastructure for administrative identity and signing; certificate custody and revocation are response concerns. | [Case 04](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/04-onnara-gpki.md) |
| KISA | 한국인터넷진흥원 | Korea Internet & Security Agency, which provides incident-response and privacy-related public services under applicable mandates. | [IR manual](../../ir-manual/README.md) |
| KrCERT/CC | 인터넷침해대응센터 | KISA's national computer emergency response function for cyber-threat coordination and incident support. | [Case index](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/README.md) |
| C-TAS | 사이버위협정보 분석·공유 시스템 | Korea's cyber-threat analysis and sharing service; apply its access and redistribution rules to indicators. | [IOC format](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/iocs/README.md) |
| PIPC | 개인정보보호위원회 | The Personal Information Protection Commission, Korea's central privacy regulator. | [IR manual](../../ir-manual/README.md) |
| MSIT | 과학기술정보통신부 | Ministry of Science and ICT; the Network Act assigns it incident-related roles alongside KISA. | [IR manual](../../ir-manual/README.md) |
| FSC | 금융위원회 | Financial Services Commission, which sets financial-sector policy and coordinates sector response. | [Case 13](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/13-financial-sector-incidents-2026.md) |
| FSS | 금융감독원 | Financial Supervisory Service, the financial-sector supervisor involved in examinations and customer protection. | [Case 13](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/13-financial-sector-incidents-2026.md) |
| FSI | 금융보안원 | Financial Security Institute, the sector security and incident-response body. | [Case 06](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/06-sgi-gunra-ransomware.md) |
| NCSC | 국가정보원 국가사이버안보센터 | National Cyber Security Center, responsible for specified national/public-sector cyber-security functions. | [Case 04](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/cases/04-onnara-gpki.md) |
| Police cyber investigation | 경찰청 사이버수사국 | Law-enforcement cyber-investigation function; evidence preservation and legal process remain distinct from operational containment. | [Evidence guide](../../forensics/evidence-handling.md) |
| Joint public-private investigation | 민관합동조사단 | A case-specific investigation team; confirm its actual mandate and findings before citing it. | [Case index](../../threat-intel/korea-incidents/README.md) |

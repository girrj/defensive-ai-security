# Threat intelligence platform (위협 인텔리전스 플랫폼)

## Definition (정의)

A threat intelligence platform (위협 인텔리전스 플랫폼) stores indicators and relationships with sources, scope and sharing controls.

## Why it matters

Unqualified feeds create noise and can spread false attribution or restricted data.

## How to do it / what to check

Ingest only permitted sources, retain confidence and TLP marking, deduplicate and expire indicators, and measure whether matches changed a response decision.

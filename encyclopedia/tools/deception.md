# Deception and honeypots (기만 기술·허니팟)

## Definition (정의)

Deception and honeypots (기만 기술·허니팟) place monitored decoy assets or credentials to reveal unexpected access.

## Why it matters

Well-scoped decoys can provide high-signal leads when no legitimate workflow should touch them.

## How to do it / what to check

Keep decoys isolated, document ownership and legal scope, log every interaction, and test that alerts reach the response team without exposing production secrets.

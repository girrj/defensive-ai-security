# Email security gateways (메일 보안)

## Definition (정의)

Email security gateways (메일 보안 게이트웨이) filter and analyze inbound and outbound mail and attachments.

## Why it matters

They can reduce phishing and malware exposure but cannot replace user identity and endpoint controls.

## How to do it / what to check

Validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC policy and logging, test attachment handling, preserve original headers for investigation and correlate clicks with identity events.

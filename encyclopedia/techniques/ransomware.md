# Ransomware and double extortion (랜섬웨어·이중 갈취)

## Definition (정의)

Ransomware and double extortion (랜섬웨어·이중 갈취) combine encryption or disruption with potential data theft and pressure.

## Why it matters

A backup reachable from production and a response limited to the encrypted host can leave recovery and notification exposed.

## How to do it / what to check

Watch for recovery inhibition and abnormal file activity, preserve evidence, isolate affected paths, verify separate immutable backups and investigate exfiltration independently.

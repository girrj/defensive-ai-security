# Supply chain and third-party risk (공급망·협력사 위험)

## Definition (정의)

Supply-chain and third-party risk (공급망·협력사 위험) arises when a partner, software component or shared identity provides a path into another environment.

## Why it matters

A narrow partner interface can still expose sensitive data or inherited privileges.

## How to do it / what to check

Inventory third-party connections and data, enforce least privilege and short-lived access, require incident contacts, monitor cross-boundary use and rehearse joint scoping.

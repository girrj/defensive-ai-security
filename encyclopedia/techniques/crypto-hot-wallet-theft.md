# Hot wallet and key custody (핫월렛·키 보관)

## Definition (정의)

Hot wallet theft (핫월렛 탈취) is unauthorized movement of assets from an online signing environment or its keys.

## Why it matters

A withdrawal can be irreversible once broadcast, so monitoring needs independent pre-release controls.

## How to do it / what to check

Keep the online balance minimal, protect signing in reviewed hardware, separate approval from execution, allow-list destinations and alert on unfamiliar addresses or unusual volume.

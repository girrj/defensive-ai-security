# Threat hunting (위협 헌팅)

## Definition (정의)

Threat hunting (위협 헌팅) is a time-bounded search for a testable threat hypothesis using available telemetry.

## Why it matters

It finds gaps that alerts and static indicators miss, including low-noise persistence and unusual identity use.

## How to do it / what to check

Define hypothesis, affected assets and period; validate source coverage; compare against a peer baseline; preserve raw evidence; turn useful findings into detections.

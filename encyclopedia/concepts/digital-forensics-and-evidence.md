# Digital forensics and evidence handling (디지털 포렌식·증거 처리)

## Definition (정의)

Digital forensics (디지털 포렌식) is the repeatable collection, preservation and analysis of digital evidence with documented limits.

## Why it matters

Rebuilding a host or losing log retention can make the timeline and affected-data count unknowable.

## How to do it / what to check

Record collector, time, source, method and hash; separate originals from working copies; account for clock skew and retention gaps; restrict access to personal data in evidence.

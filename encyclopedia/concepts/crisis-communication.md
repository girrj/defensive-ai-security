# Crisis communication and regulator reporting (위기 소통·신고)

## Definition (정의)

Crisis communication (위기 소통) coordinates accurate internal, customer and regulator updates during an incident.

## Why it matters

Unverified numbers or a missed legal trigger can harm affected people and weaken trust.

## How to do it / what to check

Keep a dated fact log, distinguish confirmed impact from hypotheses, assign approval and spokesperson roles, assess each reporting/notice duty under the law in force, and issue corrections as facts change.

# SOC operating model: tiers, shifts, metrics (보안관제 운영 모델)

## Definition (정의)

A SOC operating model assigns monitoring, investigation, response and engineering work to named roles and shifts. Tiers are a common staffing pattern, not a mandatory hierarchy.

## Why it matters

Unowned queues and handovers turn a detected event into a missed incident.

## How to do it / what to check

Define intake and escalation ownership, on-call authority, shift handover, telemetry coverage and measures such as time to triage alongside decision quality. Review aged alerts and reopened incidents.

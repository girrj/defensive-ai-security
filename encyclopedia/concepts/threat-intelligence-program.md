# Running a threat intelligence program (위협 인텔리전스 운영)

## Definition (정의)

A threat intelligence program (위협 인텔리전스 운영) collects and analyzes information to answer defined defensive decisions.

## Why it matters

Unscoped feeds create indicator volume without improving detection or response.

## How to do it / what to check

Set intelligence requirements, record source and confidence, separate incident from campaign indicators, validate decay, share under handling rules, and measure decisions changed.

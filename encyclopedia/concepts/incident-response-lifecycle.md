# Incident response lifecycle (침해사고 대응 절차)

## Definition (정의)

Incident response (침해사고 대응) coordinates preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery and improvement across the organization.

## Why it matters

Fast containment without evidence or a recovery plan can damage scope analysis and leave the entry path open.

## How to do it / what to check

Assign incident command, preserve volatile data and logs, assess scope and legal duties, approve containment, remove the cause, verify restored services and record lessons.

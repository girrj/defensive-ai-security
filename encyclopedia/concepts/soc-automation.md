# SOC automation and AI-assisted analysis (관제 자동화·AI 활용)

## Definition (정의)

SOC automation (관제 자동화) collects context or executes a bounded response action under a reviewed playbook; AI output remains advisory evidence.

## Why it matters

Unchecked automation can close true incidents, leak evidence or disrupt systems.

## How to do it / what to check

Automate repeatable enrichment first; verify source and schema; keep logs and rollback; require human approval for containment, deletion and external communication; evaluate false positives.
